# Scripture terminology and rights gate

## Product language policy

Chinese output follows the terminology and book names of the Simplified Chinese
`新标点和合本，神版` (`CUNPSS-神`). English output follows *The Holy Bible, English
Standard Version®*, Text Edition: 2025 (`ESV 2025`). These identifiers describe the
terminology baseline; they do not imply endorsement by either publisher.

For Chinese output:

- use `神`, not `上帝`, for the selected Shen Edition;
- as a product and church-style rule, allow both `他` and `祂` when context makes the
  referent clear; an exact licensed quotation must preserve the source edition's glyph;
- use Simplified Chinese book names and preserve explicit chapter-and-verse numbers;
- do not reconstruct, complete, or invent verse text that the speaker did not say.

The production glossary contains names and short non-expressive theological terms, not
an offline Bible text. Public test fixtures use original sentences or paraphrases. A
machine-generated translation is a live listening aid and must not be presented as an
authoritative Bible quotation.

## Distribution gate

Neither CUNPSS-神 nor ESV full text may be bundled, downloaded, cached as a corpus, used
as a hidden retrieval source, or copied into test fixtures until the corresponding
rights holder has approved the exact commercial App Store use in writing.

Before enabling verse retrieval or distributing publisher text:

1. Obtain Hong Kong Bible Society permission covering digital use, an App Store app,
   the intended territories, testing, caching, and any offline distribution. The public
   contact is [info@hkbs.org.hk](mailto:info@hkbs.org.hk); the
   [Digital Bible Library](https://library.bible/) is an additional licensed-content
   route for eligible organizations.
2. Obtain Crossway digital permission covering ESV 2025 and this product's
   English-to-Chinese workflow. The standard ESV API and quotation policy are not a
   substitute for that permission because their ordinary terms restrict caching,
   commercial use, and translation into another language.
3. Record the signed grant, approved copyright notice, territories, version, expiry,
   reporting duties, and revocation procedure in the private release evidence set.
4. Have pastoral reviewers approve a bilingual theological qualification corpus whose
   distribution rights are documented separately from the Bible-text license.

No App Store build may claim licensed Scripture integration while either written grant
is missing. Terminology alignment and book-name mapping may ship without embedding the
copyrighted text.
